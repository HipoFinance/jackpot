# Hipo Jackpot v1

**Status:** approved (amended 2026-10-27)

## Goal

A contract where players deposit GRAM or hGRAM, keep their full deposit, and every validation round three
of them, drawn at random in proportion to their deposit, win that round's staking yield. A share of each
round's new yield goes to the HPO burn.

## In plain terms

Players put GRAM or hGRAM into the Jackpot. The Jackpot keeps everything as hGRAM, Hipo's staking token,
which grows in value every validation round (about every 18 hours). It remembers how much GRAM each player
put in. The difference between what its hGRAM is worth and what it owes the players is the prize.

After every validation round, once Hipo has booked that round's reward, a draw runs:

- 10% of the round's new yield goes to the HPO burn. A prize that found no winner and was carried into
  this draw is not burned a second time.
- The rest is split 50% / 30% / 20% between three winners.
- Each winner is drawn separately, with a chance equal to their share of the eligible deposits, so the
  same player can win more than one prize in a round. Splitting money across several wallets changes
  nothing. Winners are paid automatically, in hGRAM, with nothing to claim.

Withdrawals are always open and instant: a player gets back hGRAM worth exactly the GRAM they deposited,
at the moment they withdraw. They then hold hGRAM and can keep it or unstake it with Hipo as usual.

**Joining late.** A deposit takes part only in draws whose period started after it was booked. Someone who
deposits mid-round waits for the next round. Their deposit still earns during the round they waited, and
that yield goes to the prize the others draw for. A player who withdraws drops out of the current draw
at once.

**How a winner is found.** The Jackpot keeps a running count of every deposit that is still in, in a tree
of small contracts. A draw picks a random position among the eligible deposits and walks down the tree to
it, so it lands on money that is really there. Money that has left takes no space in later draws.

**Withdrawing while a draw is being settled.** A draw takes a few minutes from its start until its three
winners are paid. During those minutes the deposits taking part keep their places, so that nobody who has
seen the random number can move a prize onto their own deposit by withdrawing. A player who withdraws in
those minutes still leaves at once, but their place stays empty in that one draw, and a draw that lands on
it tries again. They pay a small extra fee for those retries, in proportion to their share of the draw.

**Fair random numbers.** The random numbers come from drand, a public beacon run by a group of
independent organisations. When a draw starts, the contract fixes a drand round about a minute in the
future. Once drand publishes it, anyone can submit it, and the contract checks the signature on chain.
Neither validators nor the Hipo team can know or change the value in advance.

**Who keeps it moving.** Each step of a draw is a message anyone may send. Hipo runs a small service
(the keeper) that sends them and pays their gas, roughly 0.3 GRAM a round. If the keeper stops, any player
can take over. Nothing is lost while nobody does; the prize just waits.

**What changes for Hipo.** Nothing in the Hipo contracts. The Jackpot is an ordinary hGRAM holder that
asks the treasury for its exchange rate through a message the treasury already answers. Hipo's checklist
for turning `instant_mint` off gains one step: halt the Jackpot's GRAM deposits first.

**Trade-offs and risks.**

- A player's deposit is as safe as hGRAM itself, plus the risk of this new contract.
- Hipo's governor can upgrade it, the same trust players already place in the Hipo treasury.
- If drand stops, draws wait for it; deposits and withdrawals keep working. Withdrawals made while a draw
  waits for drand pay the extra fee above, in proportion to their share.
- The tree has room for about 16.7 million bookings. Past that, new bookings are refused until an
  upgrade adds room.

**Decisions needed:** none. The choices made are listed under Decisions.

## Definitions

**Concepts**

- **Principal**: the GRAM a player is owed back. It is fixed when the deposit is booked, and is the value of
  the hGRAM booked for them at that moment.
- **Booking (a credit)**: the moment a deposit becomes principal and gets its range, after the treasury
  has quoted the rate. A deposit that has arrived but is not yet booked is *in flight*.
- **Quote**: the pair (`total_coins`, `total_tokens`) the treasury sends in reply to `provide_current_quote`.
  Its rate is GRAM per hGRAM. The Jackpot stores the latest one it received.
- **Booking index**: `b`, the number of bookings made before this one. The first booking has `b = 0`.
- **Range**: the record of one booking, kept in its own small contract, the *range contract*, whose
  address follows from (Jackpot address, `b`). It stores the owner, `length` (the principal booked) and
  `live`.
- **Ticket**: one nano-GRAM of a range's live part. A range holds `live` tickets.
- **Live part**: the first `live` tickets of a range. A withdrawal shrinks it from the end. A ticket past
  the live part is *withdrawn*.
- **Position**: the place of a ticket when all tickets are lined up by `b`, and within a range by offset.
  Ticket numbers carry no other meaning.
- **Node**: a small contract at (Jackpot address, level, index) that keeps, for each of its 64 children,
  the live tickets below that child. A level-1 node `q` has the ranges `b = 64q … 64q+63` as children.
  A level-`ℓ` node `q` has the level-`ℓ−1` nodes `64q … 64q+63`. There are 4 levels, and one level-4 node,
  the *top node*, covers `b < 64^4 = 16,777,216`.
- **The tree**: the Jackpot, the nodes and the ranges together. The Jackpot is the parent of the top node.
- **Period**: the time between the starts of two draws. A draw's *cutoff* `E` is the number of bookings at
  the start of the previous draw. Only ranges with `b < E` can win it.
- **Draw window**: from the moment a draw moves to waiting for drand until it is idle again.
- **Deferred update**: a withdrawal from a range with `b < E` that the Jackpot handles during the draw
  window. The nodes hold it apart until a message of a later draw reaches them, so the draw's positions
  do not move.
- **Slot**: one of the three prizes of a draw (0 = first, 50%; 1 = second, 30%; 2 = third, 20%).
- **Attempt**: one random position tried for a slot. A slot whose position lands on a withdrawn ticket
  moves to its next attempt.
- **Keeper**: whoever sends the messages that move a draw along. Hipo runs one. Anyone may.
- **Pot**: the hGRAM the Jackpot holds beyond what it owes, which is what a draw pays out.
- **Carried**: the prizes of earlier draws that went back into `F` without a winner since the last draw
  start that paid out a pot (rolled over, expired, or returned while the rate had fallen). They are part
  of the next pot and were already counted for the burn.
- **Burner**: the HPO burner contract (`HipoFinance/burner`) at the address Hipo's treasury already pays.
- **drand quicknet**: the drand network with a round every 3 s, signatures in G1 and the public key in G2
  of BLS12-381 (scheme `bls-unchained-g1-rfc9380`).

**Formula parameters**

| `T` | hGRAM the Jackpot holds, by its own count | nano-hGRAM | ≥ 0 |
| `I` | hGRAM received but not yet booked (in flight) | nano-hGRAM | ≥ 0 |
| `O` | hGRAM set aside and not yet sent: prizes of the running draw, burn not yet sent, bounced payouts held for `claim` | nano-hGRAM | ≥ 0 |
| `F` | free hGRAM: `T − I − O` | nano-hGRAM | ≥ 0 |

| `L_lo` | live tickets of ranges with `b < K_next` | tickets (nano-GRAM) | ≥ 0, exact |
| `L_hi` | live tickets of ranges with `b ≥ K_next` | tickets (nano-GRAM) | ≥ 0, exact; `L_lo + L_hi ≤ P` |

| `cap` | the most a mint may book for a pending record | nano-hGRAM | `floor(coins·TT/TC)` at the stored quote; `coins` before any quote |

| `carried` | prizes gone back into `F` since the last start that paid out a pot | nano-hGRAM | exact sum; reset to 0 by such a start |
| `pot` | hGRAM a draw pays out | nano-hGRAM | `F − ceil(P·TT/TC)`; a draw needs `pot ≥ m` |
| `b_share` | burn share | basis points | governor-set, 0 ≤ `b_share` ≤ 2000; initial 1000 |
| `m` | smallest pot that may start a draw | nano-hGRAM | governor-set |
| `burn` | hGRAM sent toward the burner from a draw | nano-hGRAM | `floor(max(pot − carried, 0)·b_share/10000)` |
| `p0`, `p1`, `p2` | the three prizes | nano-hGRAM | `rest = pot − burn`; `p0 = floor(rest·5000/10000)`, `p1 = floor(rest·3000/10000)`, `p2 = rest − p0 − p1` |
| `seed` | drand randomness of the fixed round | 256 bits | `sha256(signature)` |
| `k(i,j)` | position tried for slot `i`, attempt `j` | tickets | `uint256(sha256(seed ‖ uint8 i ‖ uint8 j)) mod draw_L` |
| `R` | the drand round a draw waits for | round number | smallest `n` with `G + (n−1)·3 ≥ now + D` |
| `G` | quicknet genesis time | unix seconds | constant, from `api.drand.sh/v2/beacons/quicknet/info` |
| `D` | how far ahead the drand round is fixed | seconds | constant 60 |
| `A` | attempts per slot before its prize rolls over | count | constant 16 |

(The burn share was called `b` before this amendment; it is `b_share` here because `b` is now the booking
index.)

**Why the rounding goes the way it does.** Every rounding favours the pool:

- a booking rounds principal down;
- a withdrawal rounds hGRAM down;
- the pot rounds what is kept for principal up;
- the surcharge rounds up.

Then the solvency invariant `F·TC ≥ P·TT` (the free hGRAM covers all principal at the latest quote)
holds after each step, as long as the rate does not fall:

- **Booking:** `F` grows by `t` and `P` by at most `t·TC/TT`.
- **Withdrawal:** `F` falls by at most `x·TT/TC` and `P` by exactly `x`.
- **Draw:** `F` drops to `ceil(P·TT/TC)`.
- **A new quote at a higher rate** only makes the left side larger.

The `min` in `w` is there for the one case where the invariant can break, a rate that falls. Then every
withdrawal takes its pro-rata share of `F` instead of more than its share.

**Why `mod draw_L` is fair.** The hash is 256 bits and `draw_L ≤ P < 2^120`, so the bias of the remainder is
below 2^−136.

**Why the minimum has a 1% margin.** A deposit of exactly `min_deposit` GRAM mints tokens rounded down, and
a rate rise between the mint and the quote can take a little more off. The margin lets such a deposit book
instead of coming back.

## Context

- **The rate on chain.** The treasury answers `provide_current_quote` with `take_current_quote`, carrying
  `total_coins`, `total_tokens` and the caller's `custom_payload` unchanged
  (`contract/contracts/treasury.fc:754`, schema `contract/contracts/schema.tlb:184`). The reply goes to the
  sender, so a reply the Jackpot receives always answers its own request. The rate only rises: minting and
  burning round toward the pool, and a loss goes to `deficit` instead of lowering it
  (`contract/docs/architecture.md`, "Dead shares" and the deficit note).
- **Depositing GRAM.** `deposit_coins` with `owner` = none mints to the sender (`treasury.fc:380`). With
  `instant_mint` on, as in production, it mints at once. Otherwise the deposit becomes a bill and mints at
  the end of a round.
- **Learning that tokens arrived.** On a mint, the owner's wallet sends `transfer_notification` with the
  same `query_id`, the token amount, and the owner itself as the sender (`contract/contracts/wallet.fc:105`).
  On an ordinary transfer, the notification names the sending user, and is sent only if the transfer
  carried a `forward_ton_amount` (`wallet.fc:60-100`). So the Jackpot can tell its own mints from players'
  hGRAM deposits by the sender. Anyone can also mint to the Jackpot, with `deposit_coins(owner = Jackpot)`
  and a `query_id` of their choosing; with `instant_mint` off, the Jackpot's own `query_id` is public on
  chain before its bill mints.
- **The round clock.** Contracts can read config parameter 34, the current validator set, on chain. The
  treasury does this with `get_vset_times` (`treasury.fc:771`).
- **The burner.** It already swaps any hGRAM that reaches its wallet into HPO on DeDust and burns the HPO
  (`burner/contracts/burner.fc:536`). A swap needs about 0.5 GRAM of gas, carried with the transfer
  (`burner/contracts/imports/constants.fc:136-167`).
- **The drand verifier.** A third-party Tolk verifier for quicknet exists in `tolk-labs/verifier-registry`.
  It measured 139,513 gas in a sandbox on real rounds and is unaudited. The TVM's `BLS_VERIFY` cannot check
  drand, because it uses a different domain separation tag and the opposite groups. Research notes: the
  person's learning journal, "What are drand and the TVM's BLS instructions".
- **Message order.** TON delivers the internal messages from one account to another in the order they
  were sent. The draw relies on this for each parent and child pair in the tree.
- **Limits checked on mainnet (2026-10-27).** Config 43 `max_acc_state_cells` = 65,536. Config 21:
  basechain gas limit 1,000,000 per transaction, gas price 400 nano-GRAM per unit.

## Approach

### Contracts

1. **`jackpot`**, one instance. It holds all hGRAM in its own hGRAM wallet. It stores:
   - a storage version number, first;
   - the counters `T`, `I`, `O`, `P`, `n`, `L_lo`, `L_hi`, `carried`;
   - the latest quote;
   - the draw state, with `E` and `draw_L`;
   - the pending GRAM deposits (a small dict keyed by `query_id`, holding the player, `coins` and the time,
     emptied as mints arrive);
   - the governor and halter, set to Hipo's;
   - `b_share`, `m`, `min_deposit`, and `K_next`.

   Its storage does not grow with the number of players or bookings.
2. **`node`**, one per 64 children, addressed by (jackpot, level, index). It stores, for each child:
   - `committed`: the live tickets below that child, as this node has applied them;
   - `pending`: withdrawals held back for a running draw.

   It also stores `pending_draw`, the draw those belong to. It is deployed with a storage reserve for at
   least 10 years, and anyone may top it up. About 12 cells.
3. **`range`**, one per booking, addressed by (jackpot, `b`). It stores the owner, `length` and `live`. It
   never deletes itself, so that a draw that lands on it gets an answer. It is deployed with a storage
   reserve paid from the deposit fee.

**Who pays for nodes.** The first node of each level is deployed with the Jackpot. After that, the booking
whose `b` is the first child of a node opens it. Every deposit fee carries a node share: 1/64 of a
level-1 node's deploy and reserve, plus 1/4096 of a level-2 node's, and so on. The Jackpot keeps the share
in its float and pays each new node from it.

### Rules every message follows

These hold everywhere below. Several of them are what stop theft, so they are part of the design, not
implementation details.

- **Who may send what.**
  - `transfer_notification` is accepted only from the Jackpot's own hGRAM wallet.
  - `take_current_quote` is accepted only from the treasury.
  - A range accepts its one-time setup only from the Jackpot; a second setup throws.
  - A range accepts `check` only from its level-1 node, `node(1, floor(b/64))`.
  - A node accepts `update` and `descend` only from its parent: the top node only from the Jackpot, any
    other node only from `node(ℓ+1, floor(q/64))`.
  - The Jackpot accepts `withdrawn`, `hit` and `miss` only from `range_addr(b)` for the `b` the message
    carries, and `lost` only from the node the message names.
  - Bounced payouts are accepted only from the Jackpot's own hGRAM wallet.
  - `gas_excess` counts as a payout's success only when it comes from the recipient's hGRAM wallet,
    `wallet_addr(owner)`. The Jackpot stores Hipo's wallet code and parent to compute that address; the
    governor can update both.
- **Basechain only.** A depositor must be a basechain address, because Hipo's wallet refuses to pay to any
  other chain (`contract/contracts/wallet.fc:49`). Other depositors get their deposit back at once.
- **Every entry message pays its whole chain.**
  - `deposit`, an hGRAM deposit, `withdraw`, `start_draw`, `submit_randomness`, `locate`, `requote`,
    `expire` and `claim` each carry at least a fixed minimum value. The minimum covers every message the
    step will cause, the tree's update messages included.
  - The minimum is checked when the message arrives, before any state changes. There is one exception: the
    surcharge `s` depends on the draw state, which the range cannot see. The Jackpot checks it when
    `withdrawn` arrives, and refuses by throwing, which bounces to the range and restores `live`.
  - Every outgoing payout attaches a fixed value. Nothing an outsider starts is paid from the Jackpot's
    own GRAM.
- **Handlers of replies never throw.**
  - These handlers act only on records the Jackpot made itself, and when something is wrong they refund,
    return or hold the value instead of throwing: `take_current_quote`, the mint notification, `hit`,
    `miss`, `lost`, the bounces, `gas_excess`.
  - A node never throws on `update`.
  - A throw there would lose state, because the treasury's reply and Hipo's mint notification are not
    bounceable (`contract/contracts/treasury.fc:765`, `wallet.fc` `tokens_minted`).
  - A reply whose `query_id` is unknown, or no longer current, is ignored.
- **Quotes are kept in a dict.** Each quote request stores its purpose under a fresh `query_id`:
  booking (player, `t`), withdrawal (owner, `b`, `x`), or draw start. The reply finds the record by
  `query_id`, and `custom_payload` is not used.
  - **A record is used once.** It is deleted when its first reply is handled. A reply with no live record
    is ignored.
  - **`requote`.** If no reply has come 10 minutes after a request, anyone may send `requote(query_id)`
    with the fee. The record moves to a fresh `query_id` and the old one is void, so a late reply to the
    old request is ignored.
  - **Bounces.** A bounced quote request is acted on only if its `query_id` is still live.
- **Payouts are recorded until they are confirmed.** Each outgoing hGRAM transfer is sent bounceable from
  the Jackpot's wallet, with `return_excess` = the Jackpot and a fresh `query_id`. That covers withdrawals,
  prizes, claims, burns, and tokens returned to a player. The record `query_id → (owner, amount)` is kept
  until one of these arrives:
  - **`gas_excess` from the recipient's wallet:** the transfer is taken as having worked, and the record
    is deleted.
    - This message can be forged. Anyone can send the recipient 0 hGRAM with `return_excess` = the
      Jackpot and an open record's `query_id`, and the recipient's wallet then sends exactly this
      `gas_excess`.
    - A forged one deletes the record and credits nothing.
    - All it removes is `settle_failed_payout` as a shortcut for that payout. If its second hop really
      failed, the tokens are still in the Jackpot's wallet outside `T`, and the governor returns them with
      `return_tokens`.
  - **A bounce from the Jackpot's own wallet:** the first hop failed and the tokens never left. The amount
    goes back into `T` and `O`, and the record is deleted.
    - A failed burn is added back to the burn owed.
    - Any other failed transfer is set aside as claimable by its owner.
  - **`gas_excess` from the Jackpot's own wallet** proves nothing either way, and the record stays open.
    - Hipo's wallet sends one when a second hop bounces (`contract/contracts/wallet.fc`, `on_bounce`).
    - Anyone can also make it send one, with any `query_id`, by sending the Jackpot 0 hGRAM with
      `return_excess` = the Jackpot (`receive_tokens`). Treating it as a failure would let a player be
      paid twice.
    - A real second-hop failure leaves the tokens in the Jackpot's wallet outside `T`. The governor closes
      that record with `settle_failed_payout(query_id)` after checking the chain. The amount goes back into
      `T` and `O` and becomes claimable, or returns to the burn owed. With basechain-only owners and a
      fixed payout value, this is very rare.
    - The governor's check must find the bounced `receive_tokens` at the recipient's wallet, followed by
      the Jackpot wallet's `on_bounce`. A `gas_excess` alone is not evidence, since anyone can forge one.
      Settling a payout that did arrive, whose success `gas_excess` was simply dropped (Hipo sends it with
      `ignore_errors`), would pay the owner twice.
    - A record whose success `gas_excess` never arrives stays open. That costs storage only.
- **Claims.** `claim` clears the owner's claimable amount before sending it, and sends it as a new
  recorded payout, so two claims cannot pay twice. Claimable amounts are kept in two kinds: *prize* (a
  prize payout that bounced) and *other* (a failed withdrawal payout or return).

### Deposit

- **GRAM.** A player sends `deposit` with GRAM. The Jackpot:
  1. keeps the deposit fee;
  2. sends the treasury `deposit_coins` with an explicit `coins` and a fresh `query_id`. The message
     carries `coins`, plus Hipo's deposit fee, plus a fixed amount that rides on to the mint notification
     and pays for the booking. This is the burner's pattern;
  3. records `query_id → (player, coins, time)` in the pending dict.

  When its wallet reports a mint of `amount` with the `query_id` of a live record:
  - If `amount ≥ cap − floor(cap/100)`, the record is used: `min(amount, cap)` is put in flight
    (`T += min(amount, cap)`, `I += min(amount, cap)`) for the player. Any excess over `cap` stays in the
    wallet outside `T`, for the governor.
  - If `amount` is smaller, the record is not used and stays live. The minted tokens stay in the wallet
    outside `T`, for the governor.

  Because the rate only rises, the treasury never mints more than `cap` for the record's own `coins`. So
  a mint someone else sends under the record's `query_id` can neither book more than the player paid for,
  nor use up the record with dust.

  If the treasury bounces the deposit (Hipo halted, too small), the Jackpot returns the bounced GRAM to the
  player.

  Hipo sends the mint notification with `ignore_errors`, so in rare cases it may never arrive. The governor
  can then book a pending deposit by hand after checking the wallet off chain (`resolve_pending`):
  - It deletes the pending entry, so a late notification for it is ignored.
  - It is allowed only for entries older than two validation rounds.
  - It books at most `cap` for the entry, the same bound as an automatic match.

  **While `instant_mint` is off**, a bill mints under whatever `query_id` released Hipo's round, so at most
  one record per round is matched automatically (by the rule above, and never for more than `cap`). The
  others wait for `resolve_pending`. Before Hipo turns `instant_mint` off, its checklist halts the
  Jackpot's GRAM deposits, so in practice none are made.

  **Why 99% of `cap` is safe against a stale quote.** `cap` uses the stored quote, which every draw
  refreshes, so it is at most about one round old: a genuine mint is about 0.03% below `cap`, well inside
  the 1% band. Only if draws stopped for about three weeks would a genuine mint fall outside it, and then
  the record simply waits for `resolve_pending`.

  Like everything else the governor does, this rests on trust.
- **hGRAM.** A player transfers hGRAM to the Jackpot with `forward_ton_amount` ≥ the hGRAM deposit fee. The
  notification from the Jackpot's wallet, naming the player, puts the tokens in flight.
  - If the forwarded GRAM is too small, the tokens go back when the return can be paid for, and otherwise
    are held for the governor to return.
  - A transfer without a notification never enters `T`. It sits in the wallet until the governor returns
    it. The app never sends one.
- **Booking, the same for both.** The Jackpot asks the treasury for a quote. On the reply it stores the
  quote and computes `c = floor(t·TC/TT)`. Then:
  - **If `c < min_deposit − floor(min_deposit/100)`, the Jackpot is halted, the tree is full
    (`n = 16,777,216`), or the rate has fallen:** it sends the `t` hGRAM back to the player and sets
    `I −= t`, `T −= t`. Nothing is booked, so no empty range takes a booking index.
  - **Otherwise:**
    1. it books `c`, with `I −= t` and `P += c`;
    2. gives the booking index `b = n`, sets `n += 1` and `L_hi += c`;
    3. deploys the range contract, whose one-time setup carries the owner and `c`;
    4. sends the top node `update(tag, deferred = no, b, +c)`, carrying the deploy of any node that `b`
       opens;
    5. logs (owner, `b`, `c`).

  Booking what the minted tokens are worth, not the GRAM sent, means a fee charged inside the treasury can
  never make the Jackpot owe more than it holds.

### The tree

- **Updates.** The Jackpot sends every change of live tickets down the tree as
  `update(tag, deferred, b, delta)`, where `tag` is its current `draw_id`. Each node passes it on to the
  child that holds `b`, down to the level-1 node. The range itself is not told; it changed its own `live`
  already.
- **Descents.** `descend(draw, i, j, k)` goes down the same way. At each node, the children are taken in
  order, and the one whose `committed` sums cover position `k` is chosen; `k` becomes the offset within it.
  - A level-1 node sends `check(draw, i, j, k, o)` to the range it chose, with the offset `o` inside that
    range.
  - A node whose children add up to no more than `k` sends `lost(draw, i, j, k)` to the Jackpot. That cannot
    happen while the sums agree; the Jackpot treats it as a miss and logs it.
- **Holding back for a running draw.** On any message whose tag is later than `pending_draw`, a node first
  adds `pending` into `committed` and clears it. Then:
  - a deferred update adds `delta` to `pending` and sets `pending_draw = tag`;
  - any other update adds `delta` to `committed`;
  - a descent reads `committed` only.
- **Why every descent sees the tree as the Jackpot saw it.** Every update and every descent starts at the
  Jackpot and travels down the same parent-to-child pairs. Each pair delivers in order. So when a descent
  reaches a node, every update the Jackpot sent before it on that path has been applied, and none sent
  after it has.
- **Cost, to be pinned by tests.**
  - Each node hop is estimated at about 10,000 gas.
  - One attempt is estimated at about 92,000 gas across 6 messages, about 0.042 GRAM: the Jackpot, 4
    nodes, the range, and the reply.
  - A booking's or a withdrawal's update is estimated at about 40,000 gas across 4 messages, about
    0.02 GRAM, paid in its fee.
  - The largest single transaction stays far below the 1,000,000 gas limit.

### Withdrawal

1. The owner sends `withdraw(x)` to a range contract (`x = 0` means all of its live part). The range:
   - checks the owner, and the withdrawal fee;
   - checks that `x ≤ live`, and that either `x = live` or `x ≥ 1 GRAM`. At any rate below 10^9 GRAM per
     hGRAM, this makes `w > 0`;
   - sets `live −= x`, and sends `withdrawn(b, owner, x)` to the Jackpot with the rest of the value.

   If that message bounces, the range restores `live`.
2. The Jackpot checks that the sender is the range at `b`.
   - **In the draw window, from a range with `b < E`**, the message must also carry `s`, or the Jackpot
     throws. The update is deferred.
   - Otherwise there is no surcharge, and the update is not deferred.

   Then it sets `L_lo −= x` if `b < K_next`, else `L_hi −= x`. It sends the top node
   `update(tag, deferred, b, −x)`, records the withdrawal and asks for a quote. The surcharge stays in the
   Jackpot's GRAM, from which Hipo recovers the extra keeper gas with `withdraw_surplus`.
3. On the reply it pays `w` hGRAM to the owner and sets `P −= x`, `T −= w`. Until then `x` stays in `P`, so
   a draw in between cannot pay out tokens that are owed.

Withdrawals work while the Jackpot is halted, and while the rate has fallen. Anyone may top up a range's
storage, so a range that ran low can still be withdrawn from.

**Why the surcharge covers the retries.** A deferred withdrawal leaves its tickets as empty positions in
the running draw. Let `g` be the share of `draw_L` withdrawn this way. A slot then needs at most
`g + g² + … + g^(A−1) ≤ (A−1)·g` extra attempts on average, and three slots at most `3·(A−1)·g`. Each
attempt is paid by one `locate` fee. The surcharges of those withdrawals add up to at least
`3·(A−1)·fee::locate·g`. With `A` = 16 and an attempt at about 0.042 GRAM, a withdrawal of the whole draw
pays at most about 1.9 GRAM.

### Draw

The draw states are idle → quoting → waiting for drand → resolving → idle. A new draw cannot start until
the last one is idle.

1. **Start.** Anyone sends `start_draw`, with gas. It is refused unless all of these hold:
   - the draw is idle;
   - the Jackpot is not halted;
   - config 34's `utime_since` is later than the one recorded at the last start, so a new validation round
     has begun.

   The Jackpot asks for a quote and moves to quoting. If no reply has come 10 minutes later, anyone may send
   `requote`; a stale reply for an earlier request is ignored. On the reply, the Jackpot reads config 34's
   `utime_since` again; the draw records that value, the one current when the reply arrives, not the one
   at the request. Then:
   - If that `utime_since` is not later than the one recorded at the last start, the rate is not higher
     than at the last start, or the pot is below `m`, it returns to idle with nothing changed. The keeper
     tries again after Hipo books the round's reward.
   - If `K_next` is 0, or `L_lo` is 0 (nobody eligible), it records the new period without paying
     anything. It sets `K_next = n`, `L_lo += L_hi`, `L_hi = 0`, and records this round's `utime_since`
     and this rate. The pot rolls over, and `carried` is unchanged.
   - Otherwise it:
     1. computes `pot`, `burn` (on `pot − carried`) and the three prizes, sets `O += pot`, and sets
        `carried = 0`;
     2. adds `burn` to the burn owed;
     3. fixes `R`;
     4. increments `draw_id`;
     5. sets `E = K_next` and `draw_L = L_lo`;
     6. then sets `K_next = n`, `L_lo += L_hi` and `L_hi = 0`, and records this round's `utime_since` and
        this rate;
     7. moves to waiting for drand. The draw window opens.

   A bounced quote request returns to idle, but only if its `query_id` is the draw's live one.

   A rate rise caused only by rounding, or by someone's `gift_coins`, may start a draw early. That is
   harmless: the pot must still reach `m`, eligibility still lags one period, and any yield not yet booked
   goes to the next draw.
2. **Randomness.** After drand publishes `R`, anyone sends `submit_randomness(signature, witness)`. The
   witness is the off-chain quotient and remainder for the two 512-bit modular reductions of hash-to-curve.
   The Jackpot checks, in this order:
   1. the witness;
   2. hash-to-G1 of `sha256(R as 8 bytes)` under the quicknet tag, with `BLS_MAP_TO_G1` ×2 and `BLS_G1_ADD`;
   3. that the signature is in G1 and is not zero;
   4. `BLS_PAIRING` of (signature, G2 generator) and (−H, the quicknet public key).

   On success it stores `seed` and moves to resolving, with every slot at attempt 0. A failed check throws,
   so the sender pays. There is no timeout and no other source: a draw waits for drand however long it is
   down.
3. **Finding each winner.** For a slot at attempt `j`, the position is `k(i,j)`.
   1. The keeper sends `locate(i)`. The Jackpot computes `k(i,j)` itself and sends
      `descend(draw_id, i, j, k)` to the top node. The keeper names nothing but the slot.
   2. The descent ends at a range with offset `o`. If `o < live`, the range answers
      `hit(draw, i, j, k, b, owner)`. Otherwise it answers `miss(draw, i, j, k, b)`.
   3. The Jackpot acts on a reply only if all of these hold:
      - the sender is `range_addr(b)` (or, for `lost`, the node it names);
      - `b < E`;
      - the draw and `(i, j)` are the current ones;
      - slot `i` is still open;
      - `k == k(i, j)`.

      Any other reply is ignored.
   4. On a `hit` it closes the slot and pays the prize to the owner: `O −= p`, `T −= p`, recorded as a
      payout. On a `miss` or `lost` the slot moves to attempt `j+1`.

   After `A` misses, the slot's prize rolls over: `O −= p`, `carried += p`.
   - Only the Jackpot can start a descent, and it chooses `k`, so nobody can make a range answer about a
     position of their choosing.
   - A slot closes on its first `hit`, so a second `hit` for the same slot is ignored.
4. **Stuck slots.** A slot not settled one validation round (config 15 `validators_elected_for`) after the
   randomness was stored may be expired by anyone. Its prize rolls over: `O −= p`, `carried += p`. This
   covers a range or node that ran out of storage. It cannot be used to cheat: it only fires when nobody
   has located the slot, and the Hipo keeper always does.
5. **Done.** When all three slots are paid or rolled over, the draw is idle and the draw window closes. The
   burn owed is sent to the burner once it reaches the governor-set batch size, as an hGRAM transfer with
   `forward_ton_amount` = 0.5 GRAM. The burner acts only on the transfer notification, and pays its swap
   from that forwarded GRAM (`burner/contracts/burner.fc:536`).

**Why the cutoff makes late joining impossible.** Booking indexes are given in order. A range booked after
draw `k−1` starts has `b ≥ K_next`, as `K_next` was set at that start. That `K_next` becomes draw `k`'s `E`,
and `draw_L` counts only ranges with `b < E`. Positions line up by `b`, and every range with `b < E` comes
before every range with `b ≥ E`. So the first `draw_L` positions are exactly the eligible tickets, and every
`k < draw_L` lands on one of them. The Jackpot also refuses any reply with `b ≥ E`.

**Why positions cannot be moved after the seed is known.** The seed is public once drand publishes `R`, a
minute after the start. From the start, two kinds of change can reach the tree:
- Withdrawals from ranges with `b < E` are deferred, so the positions below `draw_L` stay exactly as they
  were at the start.
- Bookings and withdrawals from ranges with `b ≥ E` change only positions past `draw_L`.

So no one can move a position onto their own range by withdrawing. They can only empty their own position,
which turns their own hit into a miss.

**Why splitting cannot help.** Every attempt gives each of the `draw_L` positions the same chance,
`1/draw_L`. A player's chance per attempt is their eligible tickets still live, divided by `draw_L`, however
those tickets are spread over ranges and wallets. The slots are drawn independently, with the same `seed`
and different `i`, so wins can repeat.

**Why a withdrawal costs later draws nothing.** A withdrawal outside the draw window, or one held back and
added in at the next draw, leaves the tree's sums exact. A withdrawn ticket then has no position in any
later draw. Empty positions exist only for withdrawals made during a draw's own window, and only in that
draw.

### Governance

- **The governor** (Hipo's) can:
  - upgrade the Jackpot's code;
  - set the Jackpot's hGRAM wallet address, because Hipo can migrate wallets to a new parent;
  - book a stuck pending deposit, return hGRAM that arrived without a notification, or close a payout
    whose second hop failed (`settle_failed_payout`);
  - set `b_share` (rejected above 2000), `m`, `min_deposit` (rejected below 1) and the burn batch size;
  - set the halter;
  - withdraw GRAM above the storage float.
- **The halter** can stop deposits and draw starts, and only the governor resumes them. A running draw
  still completes, and withdrawals never stop.
- **The range and node code is fixed.** Range and node contracts cannot be upgraded.
- **Upgrades follow Hipo's pattern** (`contract/contracts/treasury.fc`, `upgrade_code` and `upgrade_data`):
  - `upgrade_code` sets the new code and runs the new code's migrate step in the same transaction, through
    `set_c3`. The migrate step then loads the stored data.
  - If the new code cannot read the stored layout, the transaction throws, and the upgrade reverts with the
    old code and data.
  - The storage carries a version number, so a migration knows which layout it reads.
- **Deploy checks.** The deploy refuses `min_deposit < 1` and `b_share > 2000`, the same bounds as the
  setters.

### A falling rate

If a quote is lower than the stored one, the Jackpot stores it anyway, because the lower rate is the truth.
It also raises a flag. While the flag is up:

- no draw starts;
- a prize won in the running draw goes back into `F` instead of being paid or held: `O −= p`,
  `carried += p`;
- the burn owed is held, not sent;
- new bookings are refused and their tokens returned, so a newcomer's tokens never back older players'
  losses;
- withdrawals keep paying, with the `min` in `w` sharing the loss pro-rata;
- `claim` still pays *other* claimable amounts (failed withdrawal payouts and returns), which are owed in
  hGRAM already counted. *Prize* claimable amounts wait until the flag is cleared.

Only the governor clears the flag.

The current treasury cannot lower the rate. A borrower default goes to `deficit`, which the quote does not
carry, so this guard is for a future treasury change, not for a default.

### Rejected alternatives

- **Unstaking everything each round:** gas twice over, delays while the treasury lacks liquid GRAM, money
  that earns nothing in between, and swings in Hipo's lendable amount.
- **A hook in the treasury:** a mainnet treasury upgrade for nothing the quote and config 34 don't give.
- **Commit-reveal plus a masterchain block hash:** the team holds the secret and runs validators.
- **TON's block random:** chosen by the validator that builds the block.
- **One contract holding every balance:** a TON account's storage is capped, which caps the number of
  players.
- **Ticket numbers with withdrawn gaps, and a redraw on a gap (the first version of this spec):**
  - A withdrawn ticket stays below every later cutoff forever.
  - One large deposit and withdrawal makes nearly every attempt miss and rolls the prizes over for good.
  - A whale can also take back, in the next draw, the yield the other players' capital earned while its
    own was out.
- **Renumbering the ticket space in epochs:** a renumbering touches every live range. Two fees from a
  whale could start one, so the keeper would pay many times what the whale pays. It also does nothing for
  the draw already under way, and it races withdrawals and draws across two numberings.
- **A sum tree inside the Jackpot:** its storage grows with every booking, toward the 65,536-cell account
  limit. Bookings of the 10 GRAM minimum, whose capital can be reused, could fill it and stop the Jackpot.
- **An index of gaps inside the Jackpot:** the same storage limit, a new entry on every partial
  withdrawal, and it needs running sums anyway, which makes it a tree.
- **Burning rolled-over prizes, or letting only the original draw's tickets win them:** this removes the
  whale's profit but not the permanent gaps. With exact draws, a prize rolls over only after withdrawals
  in that draw's own window. The player who caused the rollover cannot get back more than the share they
  gave up, so plain rollover stays.
- **A fee on each withdrawal to pay for redraws, under the first version:** a gap there cost on every later
  draw it was hit, so no one-time fee could price it. Under the tree a gap lives for one draw, and the
  surcharge prices exactly that.

- [ ] Splitting is neutral. For a fixed `seed`, these cover exactly the same positions and win exactly the

## Risks & rollback

- **drand halts.** Draws wait; nothing else is affected, except that withdrawals from eligible ranges pay
  the surcharge while the draw window stays open. Detect: a draw waiting for drand longer than 10 minutes.
  If drand retired quicknet, the governor would upgrade to another source.
- **A treasury upgrade changes `provide_current_quote`, or a wallet migration moves the Jackpot's hGRAM.**
  Every Hipo treasury and wallet upgrade's checklist must include the Jackpot. A falling quote is handled
  as above. A migrated wallet is set by the governor.
- **Hipo turns `instant_mint` off.** Its checklist halts the Jackpot's GRAM deposits first; deposits still
  pending that no mint matched are booked by `resolve_pending`.
- **A borrower default in Hipo.** It lands in the treasury's `deficit`, not in the rate. The Jackpot's
  principal is then exactly as safe as everyone's hGRAM.
- **The keeper stops.** Draws pause until someone sends the messages. Detect: no draw start within 2
  rounds.
- **A bug in the Jackpot.** It is upgradable by the governor, and the halter can stop deposits and draws
  at once. Withdrawals stay open, so players can always leave.
- **A bug in a node.** Node code is fixed. A wrong sum would show as `lost` replies or as winners that
  differ from the off-chain recomputation. Detect: any `lost` log. The governor's upgrade of the Jackpot
  could start a new tree under new node code.
- **The tree fills.** 16,777,216 bookings, then new bookings are returned. Detect: `n` above 90% of that.
- **Message order.** The draw's exactness rests on TON delivering messages between two accounts in order.
  The sandbox cannot show this, and the protocol guarantees it.
- **The governor key.** It can upgrade the code and so take the funds: the same trust as the Hipo treasury.

## Decisions

Settled with the person on 2026-10-27:

1. **Exact draws over a sum tree** of node contracts. A withdrawal made during a draw's window is held back
   for that draw only, and pays a surcharge for the retries it causes. This replaces the first decision,
   which deferred renumbering of withdrawn ticket gaps to a later version.
2. **Keeper gas** is paid by Hipo in v1, about 0.3 GRAM a round. Reimbursing keepers from the pot can come
   later.
3. **Language:** FunC, the same toolchain and reviewers as the Hipo contracts. The Tolk drand verifier is
   a reference only.
4. **Burn batch:** the burn owed is sent once it reaches 50 hGRAM (governor-adjustable).
5. **Minimums:**
   - a 10 GRAM minimum deposit, booked when `c ≥ min_deposit − floor(min_deposit/100)`;
   - `m` = 1 hGRAM.

   Both are governor-set.
6. **`A` = 16** attempts per slot, and expiry after one validation round, both constants.

## Amendments

Made on 2026-10-27, after the first approval:

1. **Exact draws over a tree of node contracts:**
   - the tree is 64-ary with 4 levels, and ranges are keyed by booking index `b`;
   - new counters `L_lo`, `L_hi`, `draw_L` and `K_next`, and the position `k = H(seed,i,j) mod draw_L`;
   - withdrawals by eligible ranges during the draw window are held back in the nodes and folded in at the

4. **Falling rate:** `claim` pays *other* claimable amounts. Prizes won while the flag is up go back into

   share is renamed `b_share`.
